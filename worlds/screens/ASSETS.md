# Screens test world: assets

All generated by us with ffmpeg lavfi sources (no third-party media); licensed under Apache-2.0 like the code:

- assets/test.mp4: testsrc2, 640x360, 4 s, H.264 (crf 30, faststart) (generated by ffmpeg; Apache-2.0). Not committed
  (`*.mp4` is ignored); make it with:
  `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=640x360:rate=30 -t 4 -c:v libx264 -crf 30 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart worlds/screens/assets/test.mp4`
- assets/picture.png: gradients source, 640x360 (generated by ffmpeg; Apache-2.0)
- assets/thumb.jpg: mandelbrot source, 640x360 (stands in for a video thumbnail) (generated by ffmpeg; Apache-2.0)
